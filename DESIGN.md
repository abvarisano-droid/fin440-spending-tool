# DESIGN.md

Spend Shadow visual spec. Builders apply this; they do not edit this file, `.streamlit/config.toml`, `styles.css`, or `core/ui.py`.

SITE: Spend Shadow (FIN 440)
ONE SIGNATURE MOMENT: On Shadow portfolio, the second KPI ("In {ticker} it'd be worth") is the only oversized number in the app. Everything else stays at the type scale below.
WEBSITE MAP: top nav (Upload, Spending breakdown, Shadow portfolio, Risk & CAPM, Timing test, Roth calculator, Proof). No sidebar on desktop. Wide layout. On a phone Streamlit collapses the nav into a slide-out menu; the header pill shows that button as a ghost pill labeled "Menu".

Chosen direction (v3, 2026-11-18): **Liquid Glass v8, light, matched to the live Ashton Capes site** (`assets/glass.css`, `v8.css`, `kronos.css` on ashtoncapesvarisano.space; spec in second-brain `Resources/design/liquid-glass-v8.md`, Ashton: one look for every product). Cool paper with three faint static glows, frosted white glass cards, a floating glass header pill, display serif for titles and big numbers, Geist for body, one cyan accent with dark ink, teal text links. v2 (warm white and green) and v1 (serif ledger on cream) are retired; their layouts, keys and rules stay.

## Experience rules (customer-experience pass, verified at 1280px and 390px)

- **Captions are full opacity.** Streamlit fades `stCaptionContainer` to 60%, which drops muted text below AA; CSS resets it.
- **Charts are transparent** on their glass card (set in `ui.chart` and CSS), never a grey box.
- **Legends wrap into columns** (2 per row on each color encoding, because Streamlit's chart theme overrides legend config), so nothing runs off a phone.
- **Age axes start at the first age in the data** (no zero, no nice rounding), so the Roth charts do not open on an empty band from 20 to 22. `ui.chart` applies this to any chart whose x field is `age`; `ui.chart_fan` sets it directly.
- **Chart toolbar hidden on touch screens**, where it covered card titles and could not be hovered away.
- **Money in tables uses the `dollar` format** (`$6,963.81`, `-$219.61`); shares use one decimal; money inputs that are whole dollars use `%.0f`.
- **Wide tables put the key numbers first and pin their label column**, and a swipe hint caption (`swipe_hint_{proof,risk,compare,merchants}`) sits under each one. The hints show only at 640px and below; desktop tables fit, so CSS hides them above that.
- **Proof table leads with Result** (`✓ Pass`), so a phone shows the verdict first; differences read `$0.00` / `-$1,779.75`, not scientific notation; the method for each check lives in an expander list.
- **KPI wells share one height per row on desktop**; on a phone they stack at natural height.
- **In-page links are ghost pills**; on a phone the Upload actions stack full width.
- **The phone menu says Menu.** Streamlit's collapsed-nav button ships as a bare double arrow with an empty `aria-label`; CSS frames it as a ghost pill and adds a "Menu" label (which also gives it an accessible name).
- **Motion:** content rises in only on navigation, because Streamlit keeps elements mounted between reruns (verified: no flicker when a setting changes); stale elements fade to 0.6 instead of snapping; inputs get the site's soft focus halo; cards and KPI tiles lift on fine-pointer hover; every button presses to 0.98. Numbers in Motion below. All of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Motion

Numbers as shipped in `styles.css`:

- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` (`--ss-ease`) everywhere
- Page enter: `ss-rise`, 420ms, from `translateY(8px)` and opacity 0; stagger 30ms per block, capped at 150ms
- Card lift (fine pointer only, inside `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`): 300ms, `translateY(-3px)` plus the lift shadow
- KPI tile lift (fine pointer): 300ms, `translateY(-2px)`
- Glass sheen on card hover: opacity 0 to 1, 160ms
- Primary pill hover (fine pointer): 200ms, `translateY(-2px)` and a deeper glow; ghost pills change edge and fill only
- Press (every pointer; touch also drops opacity to 0.88): `scale(0.98)`
- Stale rerun fade: opacity to 0.6 over 250ms
- Focus halo on inputs: 200ms, 3px teal ring at 28% (buttons and page links 35%)
- `touch-action: manipulation` on buttons, page links, the dropzone and the header Menu pill. Tap highlight is a light teal wash
- Properties: transform and opacity only for motion; colors and shadows change without moving layout
- Reduced motion: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` turns off transitions, hover and press transforms, the entrance animation, the sheen transition and smooth scrolling

No scroll libraries, no grain overlay, no parallax. Streamlit reruns make cinematic scroll a mismatch. The app is usable on a phone: columns wrap at 640px, the nav becomes a menu, tables and charts scroll inside their card if they overflow.

7. Expander **`shadow_ledger`**: per-purchase table, collapsed.
8. Disclaimer caption under the expander.

### 4. Roth IRA calculator (`app_pages/roth.py`)

1. Title. Course caption (Ch 5). Then: real return = today's dollars; income limits unchecked.
2. **`roth_inputs`**: glass card, three columns. Age/start | contribution source | rate (slider 6% to 10%, default 7%), convention, due, cap. Radio for contribution source; slider appears in-column when redirecting discretionary spending.
3. **`roth_kpis`**: three metrics: Balance at {age}, You put in, Growth. Growth value is teal.
4. Cap `st.info` only when the monthly amount exceeds the 2026 limit.
5. **`chart_roth`**: stacked area, contributions (`#3F6F9E`) under growth (`#0A716C`). Age axis starts at the first age in the data.
6. **`roth_mc_controls`**: glass card under the deterministic chart. Title "With uncertain returns". Method (normal vs bootstrap), annual vol (normal) or a caption that bootstrap uses fund history, optional target balance, run size. Keep it on one row that wraps; this is TVM under uncertainty, not a second hero.
7. **`chart_roth_fan`**: percentile fan. Outer band p5-p95 (`#C4F6F3` fill, 1px teal `#0A716C` edge), inner band p25-p75 (`#6FD9D1`), median p50 teal line `#0A716C`. X is age (tight, no padding), Y is balance. Call `ui.chart_fan` with `simulate_roth(...)["percentiles"]`.
8. **`roth_mc_kpis`**: Median (p50), Bad case (p5), Odds of the target. 32px serif, same tiles. No 44px number here.
9. Expander **`roth_math`**: closed-form FV, then the year table. Collapsed.

### 5. Risk & CAPM (`app_pages/risk.py`)

Class topic: risk/return, CAPM, SML. Needs loaded transactions. Market series is SPY.

1. Title: "Risk and CAPM". Course caption (Ch 2, 5, 6, 7, 9; not 4 or 8). Then: volatility and beta come from unit value (deposits do not look like returns); prices and the T-bill are Yahoo only.
2. **`risk_window`**: horizontal radio, not a card. "Your purchase dates" (default) vs "Last 5 years of fund history". Purchase dates replay buys via `unit_value`. History uses a daily-rebalanced mix (`lab.constant_mix_index`) so a six-month sample is not the only read. Prefer history for the presentation.
3. **`risk_kpis`**: five tiles for the default portfolio (100% VOO vs SPY): Vol, Sharpe, Max drawdown, Beta, Alpha. Wrap on a phone. Alpha's delta is the SML gap, colored by sign. Caption under the row: T-bill yield, EAR, day count. `st.info` when the window is under 252 trading days.
4. Two-column row **1:1**: **`chart_risk_return`** | **`chart_sml`**.
   - Scatter: x = annualized vol, y = annualized return, one filled dot per portfolio, SPY as a hollow ink ring on top, color `name`. Axis percents. Call `ui.chart_risk_return`.
   - SML: teal line of `risk.sml_line` (x = beta, y = expected return). Dots are each portfolio's realized return at its beta; SPY is the ring. Call `ui.chart_sml`. If realized SPY return is below the T-bill, the line slopes down: the explainer must say that is a short-window realized premium, not CAPM's expected premium, and point at fund history.
5. **`risk_table`**: glass card, title "All portfolios". Dataframe widget key `risk_stats` (a container and a widget cannot share a key). Portfolio pinned; beta and both alphas first. Swipe hint `swipe_hint_risk` (phone only).
6. **`risk_explainer`**: glass card, body type. CAPM `E[R] = rf + beta (E[Rm] - rf)`. A dot above the line earned more than CAPM asked for over this sample. SPY sits on the line (beta 1, alpha 0).

### 6. Timing test (`app_pages/timing.py`)

Class topic: market efficiency, behavioral finance. Needs loaded transactions. Default ticker matches Shadow (VOO).

1. Title: "Timing test". Course caption (Ch 11, 12). Then: if prices already reflect what is knowable, the dates you spent should look like luck.
2. **`timing_controls`**: glass card. Ticker (1/3) and category multiselect (2/3), same split as `shadow_controls`.
3. **`timing_kpis`**: Your dates in {ticker}, Median random dates, Your percentile. Value is `37th` (a percentile, not rank 37 of 1,000). Label: "Your percentile, of 1,000 shuffles". Neutral color; luck is not a gain/loss.
4. **`chart_timing_hist`**: histogram of the 1,000 shuffled end values (`#2FA59E` bars). Teal rule at the actual value, muted dashed rule at the median shuffle. Call `ui.chart_histogram`.
5. **`timing_explainer`**: glass card. 5th to 95th percentile is consistent with luck. Give the min/max of the 1,000 shuffles in the body (small steady purchases barely move). Lump-sum-on-day-one in the body, not as a fourth KPI.

### 7. Proof (`app_pages/proof.py`)

Class topic: validation. Works with sample data; still `require_transactions` so every check has a CSV.

1. Title: "Proof". Caption: key numbers are checked live against a second computation that does not use the app's own code; the workbook lets a grader redo the math in Excel with real formulas. The page lists what the live checks cover vs what the final audit recomputed by hand.
2. **`proof_checks`**: glass card, title "Live checks". Lead line: "{n} of {m} checks pass" on this data. Dataframe widget key `proof_table` (a container and a widget cannot share a key). Columns in order: Result (`✓ Pass` / `✗ Fail`, small), Check (medium), App, Difference (`$0.00` / `-$1,779.75`, never scientific notation), Independent. Swipe hint `swipe_hint_proof` (phone only). Method text lives in expander `proof_how` ("How each check is computed"), not in the table, so a phone can see the verdict first.
3. **`proof_download`**: unbordered block. Cyan primary download button, key `proof_xlsx`, label "Download the cross-check workbook". Caption: built in memory; sheets Roth (FV), Shadow (purchases, shares, value), Risk (SLOPE beta, volatility, T-bill, Sharpe).
4. **`proof_process`**: expander, collapsed, key `proof_process`. Title "How AI built this, and how we kept it honest". Drawn from HANDOFF (no transaction rows, no account numbers).

## Component rules (`core/ui.py`)

Signatures stay stable: `section(key, title=None, border=True)`, `kpi_row(key, items)`, `chart(c, key=None)`.

- `section`: keyed `st.container`. Glass card by default (`upload_source` passes `border=False`). Serif 400 h3 when `title` is set.
- `kpi_row`: keyed container, equal `st.columns`, each `st.metric(..., border=True)`. CSS makes each a glass tile with serif 400 32px values. The shadow signature metric is wrapped as `.st-key-shadow_signature` (gain, teal) or `.st-key-shadow_signature_down` (loss, red), 44px. Roth growth (third `roth_kpis` value) is teal.
- `chart`: Altair theme `spend_shadow` (also registered globally): legend bottom, no view stroke, axis labels muted `#4F5752`, grid `#E4E8E6`, domain and ticks `#DCDFD8`, view clip off so long category labels stay whole, background transparent on the glass card. Field `series`: domain Spent (cumulative) then Value in {ticker}, range `SPENT` (`#B0603A`) / `SHADOW` (`#0A716C`). Field `part`: Contributions / Growth in `CONTRIBUTIONS` / `GROWTH`. Field `category`: pinned `CATEGORY_COLORS` (14 distinct fills); the scale domain is only categories present in the frame, so empty ones stay out of the legend. If `category` is on x or y and color is unset, `ui.chart` fills the bars and hides the legend (the axis already names them). Field `name` or `portfolio`: `PORTFOLIO_COLORS` (VOO teal, 60/40 blue, BND muted, QQQ copper, SPY ink). An x field named `age` gets a tight scale (no zero, no nice). Named constants: `ui.SPENT`, `ui.SHADOW`, `ui.CONTRIBUTIONS`, `ui.GROWTH`, `ui.ACCENT`, `ui.MUTED`, `ui.PORTFOLIO_COLORS`, `ui.MARKET_NAMES`. Color encodings set a 2-column wrapping legend.
- Extra helpers: `chart_fan(percentiles_df, key=...)`, `chart_histogram(values, markers=[...], key=...)`, `chart_risk_return(df, key=...)`, `chart_sml(line_df, points_df, key=...)`. The two Risk helpers draw the market as a ring over the portfolio dots. Pages pass engine frames; they do not restyle these.
- Primary button: cyan `#34E0D6` pill with `#0A1420` ink. Secondary / "Use sample data" / "Clear data" / in-page `stPageLink`: frosted ghost pills, `#C4C9C3` edge, ink text, 44px tall. Download (`proof_xlsx`) is primary.
- `st.caption` for helper copy. Course lines with `:material/school:` are teal 500 (`[aria-label="school icon"]`); other captions stay muted. `st.info` for the empty-upload pointer and skip/cap notices only.
- Tables: header `#EEF3F5`, hairline `#DCDFD8` borders, radius 14, tabular nums.
